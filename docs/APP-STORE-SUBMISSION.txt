2. PURPOSE, AUDIENCE AND VALUE

Countrivo is a free geography and history game collection for people who
enjoy short daily puzzles and learning about the world through play. Six
games have daily boards: Country Draft, GeoWordle, Pinpoint, Flag Quiz,
Higher or Lower and Stat Guesser. Blind Pick and Speed Flags are additional
practice-only games; the daily games also offer practice. Choices receive
immediate feedback and explanations. Daily results can be shared, and
publishing a leaderboard name is optional. There are no accounts, ads,
subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid features.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS TO FEATURES

An internet connection is required. No credentials, registration, purchases
or sample files are needed. Open the app, choose a game, and select Play.
For Country Draft, choose a country, then a person and a free cabinet role;
complete five rounds to reach the result. GeoWordle accepts country guesses
and shows distance, direction and map feedback. Solving opens optional
neighbours and capital rounds. Practice offers fresh boards without changing
the daily leaderboard.

At a completed daily result, publishing a name is optional. Other players'
public names have Report / block controls on the leaderboard and shared
result pages. Names are filtered on the server. Reports reach the owner's
moderation interface; blocking hides the publisher on that browser/device.
Privacy > Forget this device removes its saved results/public names and
clears its Countrivo cookies. It is device-data deletion, not account deletion.
Support and deletion questions: countrivo@gmail.com.

Deployment/device verification of the moderation fixes is still a release
check; do not describe them as physically demonstrated until recorded.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, DATA, AUTHENTICATION, PAYMENTS AND AI

The Capacitor 8 iOS shell uses WKWebView to load https://countrivo.com.
Vercel hosts the Next.js website and provides aggregate web analytics and
performance measurements. Supabase stores game sessions/results, optional
leaderboard names and moderation data. Signed device identity supports saved
moves and daily limits. There is no current account-authentication flow,
payment provider or advertising SDK.

The native bootstrap integrates haptics, splash/status-bar behaviour, network
status/offline handling and game links. Gameplay requires a connection; the
offline screen does not provide offline gameplay. The current app does not
offer Apple sign-in or APNs reminders. Optional calendar reminders are files
that a user chooses to import into their own calendar.

Some clay illustrations are static AI-generated assets produced before
publication. There is no runtime generative-AI service in the game, and
player input is not sent to a generative-AI service by the game. Statistics,
providers and reference years are listed at https://countrivo.com/sources.
Portrait licences/attribution, flag-icon MIT notice and generated-art
disclosure are at https://countrivo.com/credits. Privacy details are at
https://countrivo.com/privacy.

5. REGIONAL CONSISTENCY

The app is selected for 173 App Store countries/regions. China mainland and
Vietnam are excluded because the local game approvals have not been supplied.
The same English gameplay features are implemented across regions where the
app is distributed. There are no regional login, payment or feature variants.
All daily boards use midnight Europe/Berlin as the reset time. Country/region
filters are player-selected options. Storefront distribution settings are
separate from the app's functionality.
