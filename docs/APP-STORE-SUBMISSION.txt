2. Purpose and audience
Countrivo is a free geography/history puzzle collection for people who enjoy
short daily games and learning through play. Daily games: Country Draft,
GeoWordle, Pinpoint, Flag Quiz, Higher or Lower and Stat Guesser. Blind Pick
and Speed Flags are practice-only; daily games also offer practice. No
accounts, ads, subscriptions, purchases or paid features.

3. Setup and features
Internet required; no credentials or sample files. Choose a game and play.
Country Draft has five country/person/unused-role choices with points and
explanations after appointment. GeoWordle gives six guesses with map,
distance/direction clues and optional neighbours/capital rounds. Daily
boards reset at midnight Europe/Berlin; practice is unranked.
Results show leaderboard context; publishing a name/optional country is
voluntary. Public publishers can be reported/blocked from standings/shared
results. Server checks filter names; the owner moderates reports. Blocking
also prevents that viewer opening the blocked publisher's shared result.
Private Feedback forms accept 5–1,500 characters without requesting an email
or name. Messages are stored with pseudonymous device ID and context, never
published to players. Privacy > Forget this device removes saved results,
public names and private feedback. Abuse-prevention records may remain as
described in /privacy. Support: countrivo@gmail.com.

4. Services, data and AI
Capacitor 8/WKWebView loads https://countrivo.com. Vercel hosts Next.js and web
analytics/performance measurements; Supabase stores authoritative game moves,
results, optional names, moderation and private feedback. Signed anonymous
device identity replaces account login. Native bootstrap integrates haptics,
splash/status bar, network recovery and game links; gameplay needs internet.
No payment provider, ad SDK or runtime generative-AI service is used. Static
clay art was AI-generated during production; player input is not sent to AI.
Sources: https://countrivo.com/sources; credits/licences:
https://countrivo.com/credits; privacy: https://countrivo.com/privacy.
