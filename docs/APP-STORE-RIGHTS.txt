PORTRAITS

authors, source links and licence notices. These include public-domain,

retains ShareAlike where applicable. A directory entry is not a blanket
licence for every third-party image or an endorsement by the person shown.

FONT
Erode: https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/erode
Official ITF Free Font License: https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl
The official Erode download contains Erode_Complete/License/FFL.txt,
Version 2.0, 17 August 2026. A fresh 21 October download matched the retained
src/fonts/LICENSE-Erode.txt exactly. That licence expressly covers websites,
apps and games, including application embedding and own-site self-hosting.
Attach the retained full licence if Apple needs an accessible text copy;
the Fontshare web page itself requires JavaScript. Modification and general
font redistribution have separate restrictions. This evidence does not
certify unrestricted redistribution of raw font files in a public repo.

STATISTICS AND FACTS
https://countrivo.com/sources names the providers and reference years.
https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use permits reuse under CC BY 4.0
unless indicator metadata states different terms, with attribution and the
other applicable conditions. The World Bank's general licence does not
automatically clear every original third-party provider. Countrivo publishes
attribution and has withheld SIPRI military expenditure and coffee/beer/wine
comparisons pending permission or source verification. No direct SIPRI
authorisation is claimed. Career/year records cite their factual sources;
job-fit points are Countrivo's editorial game judgements, not official ratings.

GENERATED ILLUSTRATIONS

They are not represented as photographs or authentic historical artefacts.
Gameplay does not send player input to a runtime generative-AI service.
